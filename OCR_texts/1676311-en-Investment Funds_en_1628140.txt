12 June 2025, 09:04 UTC, written by Jon Andrea Barblan

Our Chief Investment Office (CIO) expects artificial intelligence (Al) to be one of the most important investment
opportunities of the decade. Our CIO expects high rates of investment to soon be followed by growth in
applications, and expects companies across the Al value chain to generate more than USD 1.1 trillion in revenue
by 2027, five years after the onset of ChatGPT. Investors should focus on both listed megacaps and innovative
companies to capitalize.

One of our solutions to invest in artificial intelligence (Al) is the Xtrackers Artificial Intelligence & Big Data UCITS ETF.
This solution offers the following characteristics:

¢ Passive solution that aims to reflect the performance of the Nasdaq Global Artificial Intelligence and Big Data Total
Net Return Index

¢ Provides diversified exposure to up to 100 stocks which have exposure to themes linked amongst others to Al, Big
Data and Cyber Security from global developed and emerging markets

¢ Individual securities are capped at 4.5% and the index follows a market capitalization weighting methodology and is
reviewed on a semi-annual basis

Risks: The fund invests in equities and is thus riskier than cash or bonds. The fund focuses on a specific theme and
performance can deviate substantially from the broad equity market. The Fund is exposed to less economically developed
economies (known as emerging markets) which involve greater risks than well-developed economies. Political unrest and
economic downturn may be more likely and will affect the value of your investment. Additional information about these
