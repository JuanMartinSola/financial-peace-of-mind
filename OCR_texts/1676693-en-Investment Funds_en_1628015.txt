Capital Group Multi-Sector
Income Fund

09 May 2025, 1:33 pm CEST, written by Jon Andrea Barblan

Our Chief Investment Office (CIO) expects central banks to cut interest rates further in the year ahead,
reducing cash returns. Our CIO believes investment grade (IG) bonds offer attractive yields and diversified
fixed income strategies can also help investors sustain portfolio income. In a portfolio context, our ClO thinks
that complementing IG bonds with riskier credit can improve diversification and add to returns.

However, managing the fixed income exposure globally, flexible and actively is a very complex task and we
recommend delegating to best in class fixed income managers available.

One of our solutions to invest dynamically in fixed income is the Capital Group Multi-Sector Income Fund. This solution
offers the following characteristics:
¢ Core multi-sector fixed income strategy designed to generate reliable income

¢ Combines the power of four income-generating sectors while avoiding concentration risk

¢ Dynamically allocates capital across credit sectors while maintaining a balanced risk profile across high-quality and
high-yielding sectors

¢ Bottom-up convictions are driven by deep fundamental research

¢ Experienced and cycle tested investment team

¢ Attractively priced negotiated share classes for contracted clients
