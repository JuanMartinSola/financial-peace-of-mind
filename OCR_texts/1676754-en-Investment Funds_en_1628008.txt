Our Chief Investment Office (CIO) expects central banks to cut interest rates further in the year ahead, reducing
cash returns. Our CIO believes investment grade bonds offer attractive yields and diversified fixed income
strategies can also help investors sustain portfolio income.

Managing the fixed income exposure globally and flexible is a very complex task and we recommend
delegating to best in class fixed income managers available.

One of our solutions to invest in high quality fixed income globally is the PIMCO GIS Global Bond Fund. This solution

offers the following characteristics:

¢ High quality emphasis: Diversified, high quality core bond strategy with access to a global opportunity set, making
this an excellent building block for core bond investments

¢ Consistent long-term performance: Actively managed and has consistently outperformed a broad universe of fixed
income competitors since inception

¢ Time-tested process: With over 50 global portfolio managers, the fund capitalizes on PIMCO’s time-tested investment
process

¢ Guided portfolio construction: Top-down macroeconomic outlook and rigorous bottom-up credit analyses

¢ Attractively priced negotiated shareclasses for contracted clients

Risks: The fund invests in fixed income securities which poses a higher risk than cash and hence investors will move up the
risk curve and take e.g. liquidity, credit, currency and counterparty risk. The fund invests a large portion of assets which
are denominated in other currencies; hence changes in the relevant exchange rate will affect the value of the investment.
