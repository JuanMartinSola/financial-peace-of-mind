Investment Office (CIO) EPL “Six ways to invest in Europe” explores single-stock opportunities across six drivers
likely to influence European equity markets in the near term. While ClO maintain a Neutral stance on Europe,

more on defense; (ii) a new German government that puts a greater emphasis on fiscal stimulus; (iii) a possible
Russia/Ukraine ceasefire/ peace deal; and (iv) a trade war that typically leads to higher market volatility.
