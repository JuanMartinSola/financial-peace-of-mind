In this episode, Nikki Yeung, Investment Funds Specialist at UBS Global Wealth Management, speaks to Alfred
Murata, Portfolio Manager of the Income Fund at PIMCO. Together, they discuss the impact of tariffs on fixed
income markets, outlook on the US economy & opportunities in fixed income today.

This show captures the perspective of fund managers and their outlook on the financial markets.
