Dolphin CPN on Nvidia

14 May 2025, 07:52 am CEST, written by Federica Cinitiempo

Despite tech sector weakness and trade-related concerns, the UBS Chief Investment Office (CIO) continues to
expect resilience in US equities, supported by solid earnings and Al tailwinds. Nvidia’s post-earnings pullback
weighed on sentiment, but its strong guidance and Al-driven capex trends reinforce our confidence in Al as a
structural growth driver, even amid short-term volatility. Check the new Dolphin CPN on Nvidia.

Would you like to buy into the Al theme but you are worried about further market dips and volatility? Rest assured, it's
not too late - there are opportunities which can add stability.

The United States has made significant strides in the Al sector and CIO believes there are compelling reasons to favor US Al
companies especially in the near term. Beyond investing in ClO’s own "Al" portfolio, investors can prioritize investments
in Al semiconductors and leading cloud platforms.
