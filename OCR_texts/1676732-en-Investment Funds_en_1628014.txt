Trading idea Investment Funds

iShares iBonds ETF's

20 June 2025, 08:46 UTC, written by Jon Andrea Barblan

Our Chief Investment Office (CIO) expects central banks to cut interest rates further in the year ahead, reducing
cash returns. Our CIO believes investment grade bonds offer attractive yields and locking in current yields may
maximize returns.

One of our solutions to invest in fixed maturity ETF's are the iShares iBonds ETF's. These solutions offer the following
characteristics:

¢ Provides exposure to investment grade, fixed rate, US Dollar or Euro (depending on ETF) denominated corporate
bonds which mature in a defined year

¢ Bond like investment, but diversified with one product
¢ Transparency and flexibility of an exchange traded product
¢ Available in USD and EUR for maturities 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029, 2030

Risks: The funds invest in fixed income securities which are usually considered as carrying credit risk. Changes to interest
rates and/or issuer defaults will have a significant impact on the performance of fixed income securities. Potential or actual
credit rating downgrades may increase the level of risk. Additional information about these and other risks can be found
in the KID as well as in the prospectus of the fund. Past performance is no indication of future results.

The above information is for marketing purposes and should not be considered as personal investment advice.
